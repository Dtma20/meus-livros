```

Locked-off camera: the camera does not move, pan, tilt, zoom or cut.

Then, one at a time, a new upright origami book slides in from the open right end of the top shelf, gliding smoothly along the shelf board to the left until it stands flush against the last book of that row. The next book does the same, until the top shelf is full; then the middle shelf fills the same way, then the bottom shelf. Every new book is upright and resting on the shelf board for its whole path. The background is one uniform #14181c colour across the whole frame, with no gradient, no brighter corners and no vignette. Keep the exact paper material, lighting and colours of the first frame.
